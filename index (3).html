Our story

It started with a tired Cape house in Marstons Mills and two people who decided to fix it themselves.

We're Emily Gianunzio and Caroline Grady. Room by room, we retiled the bathroom, refinished the original hardwood floors, built the shelving, painted every wall and trim board, sided and waterproofed the exterior, built a split rail fence, and laid the walkway and patio by hand. Somewhere along the way we got engaged, and in 2027 we're getting married in the backyard we built.

Emily spent more than 2,000 hours on residential job sites alongside her father, a licensed electrical contractor. Caroline has built more than 20 large pieces of furniture and brings a background in math and finance to every estimate. Together we run Built Happy, and we do the same careful work for our neighbors.

The name honors Emily's grandmother, Deedee, whose rule for life was simple: just be happy and have fun.

We fixed our house. Now let us fix yours.
